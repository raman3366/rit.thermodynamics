# ME23312 — Engineering Thermodynamics

Lecture presentations, tutorial decks, notes, and supporting material for the course **ME23312 – Engineering Thermodynamics**, offered to **II Year Mechanical Engineering** students during the **2026–27 ODD semester**.

All materials are prepared for digital (smart board) teaching and are shared here for open, public access — primarily for students of the course, but anyone learning thermodynamics is welcome to use them.

| Unit | Topic | Course Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| I | Basic Concepts, Zeroth Law and First Law of Thermodynamics | CO1 |

**Sign convention used throughout:** work done *by* the system is positive; heat supplied *to* the system is positive. SI units are used everywhere.

## What's in this repository

- **Lecture decks** — one per session (~7–9 slides): title slide, timed session plan, content slides with derivations, worked examples, comparison tables and formula boxes, and a summary slide with recap, next-class preview, and home practice.
- **Tutorial decks** — three fully worked problems per tutorial (statement → stepwise solution → boxed answer), plus a practice-problems slide with answers and exam pointers.
- **Unit materials** — consolidated notes and reference documents per unit.
- **Resources** — steam tables, charts, and formula sheets as needed.

Files follow the naming convention `Session_NN_Lx_Topic.pptx` for lecture decks and `Unit_N_material.docx` for unit documents. Materials are uploaded progressively as the semester proceeds — **Watch** this repository to be notified of updates.

## Textbook and references

- **Textbook:** P. K. Nag, *Engineering Thermodynamics*, 6th Edition, Tata McGraw Hill. Content and problem styles align chapter-wise with this text (Ch.1 Introduction, Ch.2 Temperature, Ch.3 Work & Heat, Ch.4 First Law, Ch.5 Flow Processes, and so on).
- **Reference:** Yunus A. Çengel and Michael A. Boles, *Thermodynamics: An Engineering Approach*, 8th Edition, McGraw Hill.
